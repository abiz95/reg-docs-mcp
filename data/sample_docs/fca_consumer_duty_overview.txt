FCA Consumer Duty overview (placeholder summary, not an official text)

The Consumer Duty is a standard set by the UK Financial Conduct Authority
that requires firms to act to deliver good outcomes for retail customers.
It applies across the full lifecycle of a product, from design through to
how a customer is treated after they have bought it.

The Duty is organised around four outcomes: products and services must
meet the needs of an identified target market; price and value requires
firms to assess whether the price a customer pays is reasonable relative
to the benefits they receive; consumer understanding means communications
must be tested for clarity; and consumer support means firms cannot create
unreasonable barriers, such as making it harder to cancel a policy than it
was to buy one.
