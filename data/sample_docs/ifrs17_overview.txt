IFRS 17 overview (placeholder summary, not an official text)

IFRS 17 is the accounting standard that governs how insurance contracts are
measured and reported. It replaced the older, more permissive IFRS 4.

The default approach is the General Measurement Model (GMM). A contract's
liability is split into the present value of future cash flows, a risk
adjustment for the uncertainty in those cash flows, and the Contractual
Service Margin (CSM). The CSM represents unearned future profit and is
released into the income statement gradually, as the insurer delivers the
coverage the policyholder paid for.

Two other measurement approaches exist for specific cases: the Premium
Allocation Approach (PAA) for short-duration contracts, and the Variable
Fee Approach (VFA) for contracts with direct participation features, such
as unit-linked products.
